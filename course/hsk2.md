### Grammar

1. **往 + direction + verb:** 往前走，往左拐。
2. **A 离 B + 远 / 近:** 学校离我家很近。 With a distance: 离这儿五百米。 (Not 从, which marks a starting point.)
3. **一直 + verb = keep doing it:** 一直往前走。
4. **Place + 在 + reference + location word:** 商店在医院旁边 / 对面。 我在他们中间。
5. **过 + road or intersection = cross it:** 过了马路往左拐。
6. **…怎么走？ asks for walking directions:** 请问，地铁站怎么走？

### Dialogue

A: 请问，地铁站怎么走？ *Excuse me, how do I get to the subway station?*

B: 一直往前走，到第二个路口往右拐。 *Go straight and turn right at the second intersection.*

A: 离这儿远吗？ *Is it far from here?*

B: 不远，五百米。 *No, 500 meters.*

A: 地铁站在商店旁边吗？ *Is it next to the shop?*

B: 不是，在医院对面。 *No, it's opposite the hospital.*

### Practice activities

- **Role-play:** A lost tourist (the app) asks you the way to three places on a map the app shows. Give full directions, then ask a follow-up: 听懂了吗？
- **Blindfold navigator:** The app gives spoken commands (往前走三步，往左拐……); follow them around your room, then reverse them aloud.
- **Compass map:** Handwrite a map of your neighborhood with 东南西北 and 6 places; describe it with 旁边, 对面, 中间.
- **Odd one out:** 左边 / 右边 / 旁边 / 马路 (马路 isn't a position) · 东 / 南 / 北 / 楼 · 往 / 离 / 拐 / 米 (米 is a noun)
- **Character detective:** 边, 过, 近 all carry 辶 (movement); 拐 carries 扌 (hand: you signal with your hand).
- **Story chain starter:** 我家在……。从我家一直往……走……

### Grammar

1. **A 比 B + adjective:** 他比我高。 Never 很 after 比: 他比我很高 is wrong.
2. **How much more goes after the adjective:** 他比我高一点儿。她比我大三岁。今天比昨天热得多。
3. **更 = even more:** 今天比昨天更冷。 (更 can appear after 比 B; 很 can't.)
4. **A 没有 B (那么) + adjective = A is not as … as B:** 我没有他那么高。
5. **A 跟 B 一样 (+ adjective):** 我跟他一样高。
6. **差不多 = about the same:** 我们差不多大。

### Dialogue

A: 你哥哥比你高吗？ *Is your brother taller than you?*

B: 他比我高一点儿，可是我比他重。 *A little taller, but I'm heavier.*

A: 你们谁大？ *Who's older?*

B: 他比我大三岁。你和你妹妹呢？ *He's three years older. What about you and your sister?*

A: 我们差不多高。她比我漂亮！ *We're about the same height. She's prettier than me!*

B: 我觉得你跟她一样漂亮。 *I think you're just as pretty.*

### Practice activities

- **Role-play: choosing a phone.** You and a friend (the app) compare two phones on price, size and weight. Use 比, 更, 没有…那么 and 一样, then decide which to buy.
- **Comparison chain:** The app shows three people with ages, heights and weights; make 6 different comparisons in 60 seconds.
- **Opposites match:** Drag pairs together: 高–矮, 长–短, 胖–瘦, 重–轻, 难–容易, 老–年轻.
- **City vs city:** Compare your city with Beijing on weather, size and food, in writing.
- **Odd one out:** 高 / 矮 / 胖 / 比 (比 isn't an adjective) · 更 / 比 / 跟 / 漂亮 · 长 / 短 / 难 / 米
- **Character detective:** 矮 and 短 share 矢 (arrow, a measuring stick); 胖 carries flesh 月; 瘦 carries sickness 疒.
- **Story chain starter:** 我有一个朋友，他比我……

5. **从来没…过 = never ever:** 我从来没见过熊猫。
6. **第一次 + verb:** 这是我第一次来中国。

### Dialogue

### Grammar

1. **了 for a completed action.** With a plain object, put 了 at the end: 我吃饭了。 With a number or detail on the object, put it after the verb: 我吃了两个饺子。
2. **Negate with 没 and drop 了:** 我没吃饭。 (Never 没吃了.)
3. **还没…呢 = not yet:** 我还没吃饭呢。
4. **Verb + 完 = finish doing it:** 我做完作业了。
5. **刚 + verb (just did) vs 刚才 (a moment ago, a time word):** 我刚到。 刚才他来了。
6. **Questions:** …了吗？ or …了没有？

### Grammar

1. **Duration goes after the verb:** 我等了一个小时。他在北京住了三年。
2. **With an object, either repeat the verb or put the duration before the object:** 我学汉语学了两年。 = 我学了两年汉语。
3. **了…了 = and still going:** 我学了两年汉语了。 (I've been learning for two years and still am.)
4. **Ask with 多长时间 or 多久:** 你学了多长时间汉语？
5. **How long since you did something: duration + 没 + verb + 了:** 我两天没睡觉了。
6. **一会儿 after a verb = for a little while:** 等一会儿，休息一会儿。

### Grammar

2. **能 = able in this situation, or capable of an amount:** 我今天不能来。他能吃五碗饭。

### Grammar

1. **Descriptions with 的:** 红色的衣服; 穿白衬衫的那个人 (the person wearing the white shirt). The description always comes before the noun.
2. **穿 vs 戴:** 穿 for anything your body goes into (衣服, 裤子, 鞋); 戴 for things you put on (眼镜, 帽子, 手表).

4. **的 can stand in for the noun:** 我要红色的。 (I want the red one.)
5. **Shopping phrases:** 有小一点儿的吗？可以换一件吗？很合适。

### Dialogue

### Grammar

1. **Verb + 得 + (很) adjective:** 他说得很流利。
2. **With an object, repeat the verb or move the object to the front:** 他说汉语说得很好。 = 他汉语说得很好。 Never 他说汉语得很好.
3. **Negate the adjective, not the verb:** 他说得不好。
4. **Ask with 怎么样 or adjective-not-adjective:** 他唱得怎么样？他唱得好不好？
5. **Comparing with 得:** 他跑得比我快。 = 他比我跑得快。
6. **Taking a compliment:** 哪里哪里 / 还可以 / 谢谢. Modesty is the default.

### Dialogue

A: 你的汉语说得真流利！ *Your Chinese is so fluent!*

B: 哪里哪里，我说得还不太好。 *Not at all, I still don't speak that well.*

A: 你的发音很标准，声调也很准。 *Your pronunciation is standard and your tones are accurate.*

B: 谢谢！我每天都很努力地练习。 *Thanks! I practice hard every day.* (地 links an adverb to a verb.)

A: 昨天的比赛你们赢了吗？ *Did you win yesterday's match?*

B: 赢了！我们跑得比他们快多了。 *We won! We ran much faster than them.*

### Practice activities

- **Role-play: talent judge.** The app performs (sings, reads a poem, tells a joke); judge each with a 得 sentence. Then read a paragraph aloud and let the app judge you; reply modestly.
- **Compliment volley:** The app compliments 6 of your skills; answer each with 哪里哪里, 还可以 or 谢谢, plus one honest 得 sentence.
- **Self-assessment:** Rate yourself on 说, 听, 写, 读, 唱 and 跑 with 得 (我写得很慢。), then compare with a friend using 比.
- **HSK 2 boss level:** 30 questions drawn from all 10 HSK 2 units plus 10 from HSK 1, 3 lives; every miss returns to its unit's daily quiz.
- **Odd one out:** 流利 / 清楚 / 标准 / 比赛 (比赛 isn't an adjective) · 发音 / 声调 / 句子 / 赢 · 厉害 / 棒 / 一般 / 表演
- **Story chain starter:** 我的朋友唱歌唱得……
