# Mandarin Character Structure: Radical-by-Radical Lesson Plans

Nov 25, 2026 · @Kyle

## How to use these plans

Each unit takes about one week at 30–45 minutes a day. A unit teaches one radical (部首), 10–12 characters built on it, 20+ words made from those characters, and sentences that put them to work. Every fourth unit ends with a written review test.

In this doc, a "word" means a dictionary word (词), often two characters, and a "character" means one 字. Learning to "spell" a word in Chinese means being able to handwrite every character in it from memory.

**Daily routine (same every day)**

1. **Learn (10 min).** Read the radical note, then each character's breakdown. Say what each component means and which part hints at the sound.
2. **Write (15 min).** Write every new character 5 times while saying its pinyin and meaning aloud. Then cover the page and write it 3 more times from memory. Use squared paper (田字格) so the proportions stay right.
3. **Build (10 min).** Write each word in the word table twice, then copy the example sentences by hand.
4. **Use (5–10 min).** Write 3 original sentences that use that day's words. Say them aloud.

**Weekly schedule for a unit**

| Day | New material | Review |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Radical note + characters 1–4 | — |
| 2 | Characters 5–8 | Write 1–4 from memory |
| 3 | Characters 9–12 | Write 1–8 from memory |
| 4 | Word table + sentences | All characters from memory |
| 5 | Practice drills | Previous unit's characters |
| 6 | Unit test (closed book, handwritten) | Mark it; re-write every miss 10 times |
| 7 | Rest, or catch-up | Flashcards only |

**Spaced review.** Put every character and word on a flashcard (Anki, Pleco or paper), with the English meaning on the front and the handwritten characters on the back. Review due cards every day before learning anything new. Test yourself by writing, never just by recognizing.

**How the tests work.** Every test is closed book and handwritten. Mark it with the answer key, then count your score. Below 85%, repeat the missed characters for two days before moving on. A character only counts as known when you can write it correctly from the meaning alone, three days in a row.

## Unit 0: How characters are built

Almost every character is built from a small set of reusable parts, arranged in a few fixed layouts. Learn the parts and the layouts, and new characters stop being random pictures.

**The eight layouts (字的结构)**

| Layout | Chinese name | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Left + right | 左右结构 | 好 明 河 |
| Top + bottom | 上下结构 | 字 花 老 |
| Left + middle + right | 左中右结构 | 树 谢 班 |
| Top + middle + bottom | 上中下结构 | 意 类 |
| Half-enclosed (covered from one corner or side) | 半包围结构 | 病 这 问 同 区 画 |
| Fully enclosed | 全包围结构 | 国 回 园 |
| Single unit (cannot be split) | 独体字 | 人 木 日 大 |
| Three-part pile | 品字结构 | 品 森 众 |

**Meaning part + sound part (形声字).** Most characters, over 80%, pair a radical that hints at the meaning with a component that hints at the sound. The radical tells you the category; the other part tells you roughly how to say it. Watch one sound part, 青 qīng, change meaning as the radical changes:

| Character | Radical (meaning) | Pinyin | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 清 | 氵 water | qīng | clear (like water) |
| 请 | 讠 speech | qǐng | please, invite |
| 情 | 忄 heart | qíng | feeling |
| 晴 | 日 sun | qíng | sunny |
| 睛 | 目 eye | jīng | eyeball |
| 精 | 米 rice | jīng | refined, essence |

**Where a radical sits, and what it is called**

| Position | Chinese term | Example radical and its name |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Left side | 旁 (páng) | 氵 三点水, 扌 提手旁 |
| Top | 头 (tóu) | 艹 草字头, 宀 宝盖头 |
| Bottom | 底 (dǐ) | 心 心字底, 辶 走之底 |
| Frame | 框 (kuàng) | 口 国字框, 门 门字框 |

**Five stroke-order rules that cover most characters**

1. Top before bottom (三, 花).
2. Left before right (河, 明).
3. Horizontal before vertical when they cross (十, 干).
4. Outside before inside, then close the frame last (国, 回).
5. 辶 and 廴 are written last (这, 远, 建).

**Exercise.** Split each character into its parts and name its layout: 海, 草, 想, 进, 四, 谢. *Answers:* 氵+每 (left-right); 艹+早 (top-bottom); 相+心 (top-bottom); 井+辶 (half-enclosed); 囗+儿 (fully enclosed); 讠+身+寸 (left-middle-right).

## Unit 1: 氵 water (三点水)

氵 is the squeezed form of 水 (water) and always sits on the left. Its three dots are written top to bottom; the last one flicks up. A character with 氵 is usually about liquids, water bodies, washing or flowing.

**Characters**

| 字 | Pinyin | Meaning | Parts (meaning + sound) | Words |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 河 | hé | river | 氵 + 可 kě (sound) | 河流 héliú river; 黄河 the Yellow River |
| 海 | hǎi | sea | 氵 + 每 měi (sound) | 大海 the sea; 海边 seaside |
| 洗 | xǐ | wash | 氵 + 先 xiān (sound) | 洗手 wash hands; 洗澡 xǐzǎo shower |
| 湖 | hú | lake | 氵 + 胡 hú (sound) | 湖边 lakeside; 西湖 West Lake |
| 汤 | tāng | soup | 氵 + 汤's right part (also in 场, 烫) | 喝汤 have soup; 汤圆 sweet rice balls |
| 酒 | jiǔ | alcohol | 氵 + 酉 (a wine jar) | 啤酒 beer; 酒店 hotel |
| 游 | yóu | swim, travel | 氵 + 斿 + 子 | 游泳 yóuyǒng swim; 旅游 travel |
| 清 | qīng | clear | 氵 + 青 qīng (sound) | 清楚 qīngchu clear; 清水 clean water |
| 没 | méi | not have | 氵 + 殳 (a hand with a tool) | 没有 don't have; 没关系 it's OK |
| 汗 | hàn | sweat | 氵 + 干 gān (sound) | 出汗 sweat; 汗水 sweat |
| 流 | liú | flow | 氵 + 流's right part | 流利 liúlì fluent; 流行 popular |
| 油 | yóu | oil | 氵 + 由 yóu (sound) | 加油 refuel, "come on!"; 石油 petroleum |

Bonus words that use two water characters: 游泳 (swim), 洗澡 (shower), 江河 (rivers).

**Sentences to copy by hand**

1. 我每天早上都要洗澡。 I shower every morning.
2. 他的中文说得很流利。 He speaks Chinese fluently.
3. 天气太热了，我出了很多汗。 It's too hot; I sweated a lot.
4. 我们周末去海边游泳吧。 Let's go swimming at the beach this weekend.
5. 这碗汤有点儿咸，可是很好喝。 This soup is a bit salty, but it tastes good.
6. 老师讲得很清楚，我都听懂了。 The teacher explained clearly; I understood everything.
7. 没关系，我们明天再去湖边。 It's OK, we'll go to the lake tomorrow.
8. 这首歌现在很流行。 This song is popular right now.

## Unit 2: 艹 grass (草字头)

艹 sits on top and is written in three strokes: horizontal, short vertical, short vertical. Characters with 艹 are usually plants, herbs, vegetables, flowers, or things made from them (tea, medicine).

**Characters**

| 字 | Pinyin | Meaning | Parts (meaning + sound) | Words |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 草 | cǎo | grass | 艹 + 早 zǎo (sound) | 草地 lawn; 草莓 cǎoméi strawberry |
| 花 | huā | flower; spend | 艹 + 化 huà (sound) | 花园 garden; 花钱 spend money |
| 茶 | chá | tea | 艹 + 人 + 木 (a plant someone picks) | 喝茶 drink tea; 茶叶 tea leaves |
| 菜 | cài | vegetable, dish | 艹 + 采 cǎi (sound) | 点菜 order food; 蔬菜 vegetables |
| 药 | yào | medicine | 艹 + 约 yuē | 吃药 take medicine; 药店 pharmacy |
| 苹 | píng | apple (in 苹果) | 艹 + 平 píng (sound) | 苹果 apple |
| 英 | yīng | hero; English | 艹 + 央 yāng (sound) | 英语 English; 英雄 hero |
| 节 | jié | festival; section | 艹 + 卩 | 节日 holiday; 春节 Spring Festival; 节目 show |
| 苦 | kǔ | bitter | 艹 + 古 gǔ (sound) | 辛苦 hard-working; 痛苦 painful |
| 落 | luò | fall | 艹 + 洛 luò (sound) | 降落 land (plane); 落后 fall behind |
| 蓝 | lán | blue | 艹 + 监 jiān | 蓝色 blue; 蓝天 blue sky |
| 获 | huò | obtain | 艹 + 犭 + 犬 | 获得 win, obtain; 收获 harvest, gains |

**Sentences to copy by hand**

1. 春节是中国最重要的节日。 Spring Festival is China's most important holiday.
2. 你工作太辛苦了，休息一下吧。 You work too hard; take a rest.
3. 感冒了就去药店买点儿药。 If you catch a cold, go buy some medicine at the pharmacy.
4. 服务员，我们要点菜。 Waiter, we'd like to order.
5. 飞机快要降落了。 The plane is about to land.
6. 她每天花很多时间学英语。 She spends a lot of time studying English every day.
7. 秋天到了，树叶都落下来了。 Autumn has come; the leaves have all fallen.
8. 他在比赛中获得了第一名。 He won first place in the competition.

4. 我买了一斤\_\_\_果。
5. 你最喜欢哪个电视节\_\_\_？

C. Translate into Chinese characters:

1. I bought some flowers for my mother.
2. Do you like Chinese food (Chinese dishes)?
3. Where is the pharmacy?
4. Her English is very good.

**Answer key**

A: 草 花 茶 菜 药 苹 英 节 苦 落 蓝 获. B: 茶, 蓝, 苦, 苹, 目. C: 我给妈妈买了一些花。 / 你喜欢吃中国菜吗？ / 药店在哪儿？ / 她的英语很好。 / 谢谢，你辛苦了。 D: Accept any two, e.g. 节 (festival; the 艹 comes from 竹 bamboo joints in the traditional 節) and 获 (obtain; the 艹 comes from the traditional form 獲/穫). Lesson: a radical is a strong hint, not a guarantee.

## Unit 3: 扌 hand (提手旁)

扌 is the squeezed form of 手 (hand) and sits on the left. It is written in three strokes: horizontal, vertical hook, then a rising stroke. Characters with 扌 are actions you do with your hands: hit, push, pull, carry, hang. When 手 sits at the bottom it keeps its full form, as in 拿 (ná, take).

**Characters**

| 字 | Pinyin | Meaning | Parts (meaning + sound) | Words |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 打 | dǎ | hit, play, make (a call) | 扌 + 丁 dīng (sound) | 打电话 make a call; 打扫 clean up |
| 找 | zhǎo | look for | 扌 + 戈 (a weapon) | 找到 find; 找钱 give change |
| 把 | bǎ | hold; (object marker) | 扌 + 巴 bā (sound) | 把门关上 close the door; 一把椅子 a chair |
| 拉 | lā | pull | 扌 + 立 | 拉开 pull open; 拉手 hold hands |
| 推 | tuī | push | 扌 + 隹 | 推开 push open; 推荐 recommend |
| 抱 | bào | hug, hold | 扌 + 包 bāo (sound) | 拥抱 hug; 抱歉 sorry |
| 提 | tí | lift, raise | 扌 + 是 | 提高 improve; 提前 in advance |
| 换 | huàn | change, exchange | 扌 + 奂 | 换钱 exchange money; 交换 swap |
| 接 | jiē | receive, pick up | 扌 + 妾 | 接电话 answer the phone; 接受 accept |
| 挂 | guà | hang | 扌 + 圭 guī (sound) | 挂号 register (at a hospital); 挂电话 hang up |
| 指 | zhǐ | finger; point | 扌 + 旨 zhǐ (sound) | 手指 finger; 指出 point out |
| 拍 | pāi | clap, take (a photo) | 扌 + 白 bái (sound) | 拍照 take a photo; 拍手 clap |

**Sentences to copy by hand**

1. 请帮我把门推开。 Please help me push the door open.
2. 我找了半天，终于找到钥匙了。 I looked for ages and finally found the keys.
3. 你能帮我们拍张照吗？ Could you take a photo of us?
4. 抱歉，我来晚了。 Sorry, I'm late.
5. 我想提高我的汉语水平。 I want to improve my Chinese.
6. 他在打电话，你等一下。 He's on the phone; wait a moment.
7. 飞机几点到？我去机场接你。 What time does the plane arrive? I'll pick you up at the airport.
8. 把你的衣服挂在这儿吧。 Hang your clothes here.
9. 在中国看病要先挂号。 In China you register first when you see a doctor.

3. 他正在\_\_\_电话。
4. 我们一起\_\_\_张照吧。
5. 我想\_\_\_高我的汉语水平。

## Unit 4: 口 mouth (口字旁)

口 can sit anywhere: left (吃), top (哭), bottom (告), or inside a frame (问). On the left it is drawn smaller and higher. Characters with 口 are about eating, speaking, sounds, and the sentence particles 吗, 吧, 呢, which were invented to write sounds.

**Characters**

| 字 | Pinyin | Meaning | Parts (meaning + sound) | Words |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 吃 | chī | eat | 口 + 乞 qǐ | 吃饭 eat a meal; 小吃 snacks |
| 喝 | hē | drink | 口 + 曷 hé (sound) | 喝水 drink water; 喝茶 drink tea |
| 唱 | chàng | sing | 口 + 昌 chāng (sound) | 唱歌 sing; 唱片 record |
| 叫 | jiào | call, be named | 口 + 丩 | 叫做 be called; 叫车 call a car |
| 吗 | ma | (yes/no question) | 口 + 马 mǎ (sound) | 好吗 OK? |
| 吧 | ba | (suggestion) | 口 + 巴 bā (sound) | 走吧 let's go |
| 呢 | ne | (follow-up question) | 口 + 尼 ní (sound) | 你呢 and you? |
| 听 | tīng | listen | 口 + 斤 | 听说 hear that; 好听 pleasant to hear |
| 咖 | kā | (coffee) | 口 + 加 jiā (sound) | 咖啡 coffee |
| 啡 | fēi | (coffee) | 口 + 非 fēi (sound) | 咖啡馆 café |
| 哭 | kū | cry | two 口 on top + 犬 | 哭了 cried; 别哭 don't cry |
| 问 | wèn | ask | 门 mén (sound) + 口 inside | 问题 question; 请问 excuse me |

Other positions to notice: 告 (gào, tell; 口 at the bottom, as in 告诉), 号 (hào, number; 口 on top, as in 号码).

**Sentences to copy by hand**

1. 请问，这儿附近有咖啡馆吗？ Excuse me, is there a café near here?
2. 我听说他下个月要去北京。 I heard he's going to Beijing next month.
3. 这首歌很好听，你会唱吗？ This song sounds great; can you sing it?
4. 你叫什么名字？ What's your name?
5. 小孩子饿了就会哭。 Little kids cry when they're hungry.
6. 有问题就问老师吧。 If you have a question, ask the teacher.
7. 你喝茶还是喝咖啡？ Do you drink tea or coffee?
8. 我们一起去吃饭吧。 Let's go eat together.

## Unit 5: 亻 person (单人旁)

亻 is 人 (person) squeezed onto the left: a slanting stroke, then a vertical. Characters with 亻 are about people, what they do, and how they relate: living, resting, trusting, borrowing. You already know 你 and 他; this unit adds twelve more.

**Characters**

| 字 | Pinyin | Meaning | Parts (meaning + sound) | Words |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 们 | men | (plural) | 亻 + 门 mén (sound) | 我们 we; 人们 people |
| 住 | zhù | live, stay | 亻 + 主 zhǔ (sound) | 住在 live in; 记住 remember |
| 做 | zuò | do, make | 亻 + 故 | 做饭 cook; 做作业 do homework |
| 体 | tǐ | body | 亻 + 本 (root) | 身体 health, body; 体育 sports |
| 休 | xiū | rest | 亻 + 木 (a person leaning on a tree) | 休息 rest; 退休 retire |
| 信 | xìn | trust; letter | 亻 + 言 (a person's word) | 相信 believe; 信用卡 credit card |
| 但 | dàn | but | 亻 + 旦 dàn (sound) | 但是 but; 不但 not only |
| 位 | wèi | place; (polite measure word) | 亻 + 立 (a person standing in place) | 座位 seat; 一位老师 a teacher |
| 件 | jiàn | (measure word for items) | 亻 + 牛 | 一件衣服 a piece of clothing; 文件 document |
| 便 | biàn / pián | convenient / cheap | 亻 + 更 | 方便 convenient; 便宜 piányi cheap |
| 借 | jiè | borrow, lend | 亻 + 昔 xī | 借书 borrow a book; 借钱 borrow money |
| 像 | xiàng | resemble | 亻 + 象 xiàng (sound) | 好像 seem; 像…一样 just like |

## Unit 17: Look-alike radicals

Four pairs of radicals look nearly identical but mean different things. Mixing them up is the most common handwriting mistake at this stage, so this unit drills the differences directly.

**Pair 1: 氵 water (three dots) vs 冫 ice (two dots).** 冫 means ice or cold. Count the dots.

| 字 | Pinyin | Meaning | Words |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 冷 | lěng | cold | 冷水 cold water; 很冷 very cold |
| 冰 | bīng | ice | 冰水 ice water; 冰箱 fridge |
| 冬 | dōng | winter | 冬天 winter (two dots at the bottom) |
| 凉 | liáng | cool | 凉快 pleasantly cool |
| 决 | jué | decide | 决定 decide |
| 次 | cì | time (occurrence) | 第一次 the first time |

**Pair 2: 礻 spirit (one dot, 4 strokes) vs 衤 clothing (two dots, 5 strokes).** 礻 comes from 示 (altar): rituals, blessings, gods. 衤 comes from 衣 (clothing).

| 礻 spirit | Pinyin | Meaning | 衤 clothing | Pinyin | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 礼 | lǐ | gift, ritual | 衬 | chèn | lining (衬衫 shirt) |
| 祝 | zhù | wish | 衫 | shān | shirt |
| 福 | fú | blessing | 裤 | kù | trousers |
| 神 | shén | god, spirit | 袜 | wà | sock |
| 社 | shè | society | 被 | bèi | quilt; (passive marker) |
| 视 | shì | look at | 初 | chū | beginning (cloth + knife: the first cut) |

**Pair 3: 日 sun (tall) vs 曰 say (wide and flat).** 曰 is the classical word for "to say." It appears inside 最 zuì (most), 曾 céng (once, formerly) and 替 tì (replace). Write 曰 wider than it is tall.

**Pair 4: 月 moon vs 月 flesh.** They look the same in modern writing. On the left with a body meaning, it is flesh: 脸 liǎn (face), 脚 jiǎo (foot), 腿 tuǐ (leg), 胖 pàng (fat), 肚 dù (belly), 脑 nǎo (brain). With a time meaning, it is the moon: 期 qī (period, as in 星期), 明 míng.
